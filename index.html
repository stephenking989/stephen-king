Tools

Browser-based, free to use, no sign-up. Both are in active development, so expect rough edges.

FieldBook

In development

A digital field book for land surveyors, built around real field work and getting better all the time. Notes, points, geotagged photos, voice notes, sketches, GNSS observations and a survey-day tracker in one place, with inverse, offset and intersection calcs on hand. Work offline, and install it on your phone. Export PNEZD CSV, KML or print-ready PDF pages. Open source.

Open the app Source on GitHub

Survey Calculator

In development

Surveying calculators that run in the browser, built for field use on a phone or tablet. An angle calculator (DMS, decimal degrees and bearings, with a step log), triangle solver, simple and compound curve solvers with a stakeout table, a GNSS antenna slant-height calculator that shows its working, and an angle converter. A Knowledge page collects the formulas behind them, from curves to traverse closure.

Open the calculator Source on GitHub
